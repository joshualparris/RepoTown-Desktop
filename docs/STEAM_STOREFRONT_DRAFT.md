## Title
Repository Town

## Short description (draft; verify against real play)
Explore a lively fictional Australian town, discover shops and homes, try local jobs, meet residents, earn money and make your own adventures.

## About the game (draft)
Welcome to Repository Town, a playful alternative-universe Australian town with 188 homes, workplaces and strange corners to discover. Wander its streets, enter buildings, explore the local economy and find activities that interest you.

**Explore:** Discover neighbourhoods, follow the map, enter businesses and investigate the town's history.

**Work:** Try interactive activities, take on roles and see how money and resources affect your choices. Systems still undergoing polish must be shown in the description only after manual tests.

**Meet residents:** Encounter locals with daily routines, conversations and places to be. This is a simulated town, not a promise of human-equivalent AI personalities.

**Play your way:** Use keyboard/mouse, or a gamepad with basic left/right-stick movement and look, A jump, X interact, B back, Y map and right shoulder run. Current controller support is a development feature and is not yet Steam Deck Verified.

**Play offline:** Your progress is saved locally on the computer. In the desktop version, the Game menu can export a backup or safely restore an earlier progress file.

## Online multiplayer disclosure (must remain visible until authoritative shared world is shipped)
An experimental online mode lets signed-in players see one another in the same area. NPCs, vehicles, jobs, economy and inventories are **not yet synchronised**. Do NOT select Steam's full "Online Co-op" feature tag on this basis.
